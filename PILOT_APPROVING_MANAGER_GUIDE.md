# Pilot Approving Manager guide

## Role purpose and sign-in

Provide independent approval for movements, serious exceptions, reconciliations, findings and authorised hold actions. Sign in as Security Supervisor / Approving Manager and verify the requester/submitter is not you.

## Daily workflow and tasks

1. Review submitted movement purpose, driver/vehicle, documents, times and cargo; approve/reject with a reason.
2. Review gate exceptions and original evidence without editing it; document clearance, denial, correction or escalation.
3. Review reconciliation values/source quality and record explanation/corrective action.
4. Inspect analytics rule snapshot/supporting records; review/dismiss/reopen/escalate as a human decision.
5. Approve/return/reject a submitted finding independently; confirm subject response and contradictory evidence were considered.

## Never do

Do not self-approve, infer guilt from an indicator/discrepancy, weaken a hold, overwrite evidence/history, approve from a screenshot alone, or treat missing tracker data as proof.

## Failure and escalation

Reject/hold when authority, evidence, identity or tenant is unclear. Escalate Critical/High safety/privacy issues immediately; use Pilot Support for degraded providers/jobs/database and the incident runbook for disclosure.
