# Pilot Dispatch and Logistics Officer guide

## Role purpose and sign-in

Plan and submit synthetic vehicle movements. Sign in with the assigned local `pilot.example.test` account and confirm the Dispatch dashboard; you are deliberately unable to approve your own request.

## Daily workflow and core tasks

1. Check driver, vehicle, documents, operational status and honest tracker freshness.
2. Create a movement with site, purpose, destination, expected times/distance, sender/recipient and cargo/equipment.
3. Attach only synthetic delivery documents, verify the reference, and submit.
4. Monitor approval/gate/reconciliation status and refer a concern without assuming wrongdoing.

Required evidence is the movement reference and synthetic document/checksum. Do not put confidential allegations in movement text.

## Never do

Do not approve/reject, operate a gate, resolve an exception, mark stale tracker data live, import real people/assets, send invitations or work around a suspended/locked vehicle.

## Warnings, failure and escalation

If data is missing, tracker unavailable, duplicate movement suspected or network disconnected, stop mutation, record the safe state and contact the Approving Manager/Pilot Support. Reload authoritative state after reconnection before retrying.

## Security, sign-out and quick reference

- [ ] Correct driver/vehicle/site and tenant
- [ ] Expected return/distance and cargo complete
- [ ] Source/freshness label understood
- [ ] Submitted—not self-approved
- [ ] Sign out
