# Pilot Company Administrator guide

## Role purpose and sign-in

Configure the synthetic customer organisation and oversee access, master data, retention and readiness. Sign in only through the local URL with the assigned `pilot.example.test` account; never share credentials or use another role’s account.

## Main dashboard and daily workflow

1. Review tenant/subscription warnings, document expiry and security status.
2. Check users/roles, sites/gates, drivers/vehicles and retention settings.
3. Review active external-auditor grants and end access when work finishes.
4. Escalate provider/job/storage/readiness degradation to Pilot Support.

## Core tasks and evidence

- Maintain sites/gates and master-data configuration using approved synthetic values.
- Verify documents and record issuer/status without uploading real documents.
- Initiate export/deletion requests and preserve the second-person approval boundary.
- Grant a dedicated External Auditor only named cases, minimum download flags and a short expiry.
- Evidence: redacted audit reference, UAT case ID and safe screenshot—not passwords or confidential narratives.

## Never do

Do not approve your own deletion request, rewrite evidence, invent legal retention, enrol real biometrics, broaden a role for convenience, connect a provider, or paste credentials into notes.

## Warnings, failure and escalation

Stop if tenant identity is unexpected, a foreign record appears, a hold blocks deletion, diagnostics expose content, or storage/provider status is degraded. Preserve the safe error and contact Pilot Support; follow the incident process for suspected disclosure.

## Security and sign-out

Use least privilege, confirm target tenant before every change, avoid shared devices, and sign out at session end. Close the browser on a shared workstation.
