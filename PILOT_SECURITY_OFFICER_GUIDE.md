# Pilot Gate Security Officer guide

## Role purpose and sign-in

Verify approved synthetic movements, driver/vehicle identity, inspections and evidence at the gate. Use only the assigned account and confirm the gate/site/direction before acting.

## Daily gate workflow

1. Search the approved movement reference; never clear a DRAFT/SUBMITTED/rejected movement.
2. Start the correct entry/exit event once; confirm driver, vehicle and source-labelled identity result.
3. Complete every required checklist item and reading. Attach only synthetic evidence.
4. Raise defects/discrepancies honestly. Serious exceptions go to an independent supervisor.
5. Clear/deny/complete only as authorised, then confirm server state and reconciliation.

## Evidence and prohibitions

Evidence must show the synthetic event/item, capture time, category and checksum. Never stage a photo, reuse another event’s evidence, enrol biometrics, edit master data, approve a movement, resolve your own serious exception or claim offline completion.

## Failure and escalation

- Tablet/camera unavailable: use the documented file fallback on another authorised device or pause; never use personal messaging.
- Network unavailable: the product is online-only. Record locally under the business continuity sheet, secure the gate physically, and enter nothing until connectivity/authenticated state returns.
- Tracker/storage unavailable or upload failed: do not invent results or fall back to unprotected storage; hold the workflow and alert supervisor/support.
- Wrong driver/vehicle or duplicate event: stop, preserve state and escalate.

- [ ] Evidence belongs to this event
- [ ] Reason/exception and supervisor route complete
