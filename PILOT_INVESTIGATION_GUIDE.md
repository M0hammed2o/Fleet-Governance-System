# Pilot Investigation guide

## Role purpose and sign-in

Conduct fair, tenant-scoped synthetic investigations and submit evidence-based findings. Sign in as Internal Investigator / Auditor; the Approving Manager remains a different user.

## Investigation workflow

1. Confirm referral/source remains unchanged; triage and accept assignment.
2. Record parties as subject/witness/other—not “suspect”—and preserve their response separately.
3. Add factual notes/tasks, confidentiality level and synthetic evidence; never delete history.
4. Consider supporting and contradictory material. Draft a neutral outcome and recommendations.
5. Submit for independent approval; self-approval must fail. Generate report only after approval and close under policy.

Required evidence includes numbered held assets, provenance, relevance, chronology and safe report reference.

## Never do

Do not pre-judge, hide a response, copy restricted notes into generic messages, edit approved findings, bypass holds, access unrelated cases or approve your own finding.

## Failure and escalation

Pause on wrong-tenant/role disclosure, missing evidence, upload failure, compromised credentials or unreliable source. Preserve redacted chronology and notify the Investigation Manager/Pilot Support/privacy lead.

## Security, sign-out and quick reference

- [ ] Scope/confidentiality/assignment correct
- [ ] Response and contradictory evidence retained
- [ ] Evidence numbered and held
- [ ] Independent approval completed
- [ ] Access/report checked; sign out
