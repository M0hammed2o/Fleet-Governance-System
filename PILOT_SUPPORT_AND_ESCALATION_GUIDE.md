# Pilot Support and Escalation Guide

Stop the affected workflow and preserve safe chronology for suspected cross-tenant access, secret/biometric exposure, unauthorized approval, data loss, or unsafe gate advancement. Treat exploitable isolation/access failures, exposed secrets/biometrics, or destructive corruption as Critical; serious bypass, incorrect authorization, or repeatable safety-state errors as High. Customer handover stops for either severity.

For camera/provider failure, distinguish permission denied, unavailable, and non-match; never accuse the driver. Retry within bounds or use documented manager-approved fallback. For tracker gaps, show synthetic/imported/unavailable provenance and use manual confirmation. For disconnected clients, do not queue unapproved mutations; reconnect and reload authoritative state. Record defect ID, severity, scope, safe reproduction, candidate commit, owner, workaround, retest, and closure approval—never secrets or biometric media.
